Questions

Good to know.

Is a Galaxy from Buy Unlocked the same phone as one from Samsung.com?

A new U.S. Galaxy is the same phone wherever you buy it. What changes is the store: its price, return policy, and services like trade-in and financing. If you buy an international model or an open-box or certified pre-owned phone, check the model number and condition before you buy.

What's the difference between Samsung Certified Re-Newed and certified pre-owned?

Certified Re-Newed is Samsung's own refurbishment program, sold on Samsung.com. Certified pre-owned is a general term: phones inspected and graded by the seller or its suppliers. When we launch, each listing will state the condition and what's covered.

Will an unlocked phone work on my carrier?

Usually, but not always. Unlocked means the phone isn't tied to one carrier. It still needs the right network bands. Use our Will it work? checker, then confirm with your carrier's IMEI check.
