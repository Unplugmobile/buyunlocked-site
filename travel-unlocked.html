Questions

Good to know.

When can I buy Travel Unlocked?

Travel Unlocked is in development. Join the waitlist below and we'll email you when it launches, along with final pricing and the destinations covered.

Do I need to change my carrier?

No. The phone is unlocked, so you can keep your home carrier and plan. The travel eSIM adds a separate data line for your trip.

Does the travel eSIM include a phone number?

Travel eSIMs are typically data-only. You can call and message over apps like WhatsApp, FaceTime and Zoom, and keep your home number on your main line.

Do I have to pay for translation?

No. Live translation is built into the phone, so there are no translation minutes to buy and no subscription.

Do the earbuds need an app?

No. They pair with your phone like any Bluetooth earbuds and work for translation, music and calls.

Can I use my own phone?

If your phone is unlocked and supports eSIM, it may work with the eSIM and earbuds. Use our compatibility checker to see whether your phone works on US carriers.
