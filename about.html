About us

Freedom is the feature.

Buy Unlocked sells unlocked phones and Unlocked bundles that work on the carrier you choose, at home or abroad. We believe your phone should work for you, not lock you in.

Who we are

Unlocked is a movement.

Buy Unlocked is a brand of LEK Telecom LLC, a mobile devices and services company based in Denver, Colorado. Our team has years of experience in the mobile industry, working with phone makers, carriers, distributors and accessory brands.

We source new, open-box and certified pre-owned unlocked phones from established distributors, and we build bundles around them. Each Unlocked bundle pairs a phone with the gear and services to do something new: stay powered all day, game longer, take a break from the screen, or travel without roaming bills.

We're launching direct to customers at buyunlocked.net and on major U.S. marketplaces, with orders shipped from the United States.
