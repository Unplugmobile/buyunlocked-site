Why unlocked

Freedom is the feature.

A locked phone ties you to one carrier. An unlocked phone works for you. Here's what that gets you.

01

Pick your carrier

Use the network and plan that fit your life, prepaid or postpaid. Check your phone.

02

Switch anytime

Better deal somewhere else? Move your number and keep your phone.

03

Travel without a lock

Pop in a local SIM or travel eSIM and skip the roaming charges.

04

Own what you paid for

No contract strings. Your phone keeps its value when you sell or trade it.
