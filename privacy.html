Privacy policy

What we collect on buyunlocked.net, why, and what you can do about it. The short version: an email address if you give us one, basic analytics, and nothing sold.

This policy covers buyunlocked.net and its pages, including gg.buyunlocked.net. The site is run by LEK Telecom LLC, a Colorado company. Buy Unlocked is a brand of LEK Telecom LLC. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean LEK Telecom LLC.

What we collect

Your email address, if you join our list. When you sign up for launch updates, we collect the email address you enter. The list is run by our email provider, Zoho Campaigns, which sends a confirmation email before you are added.

Analytics. We use Google Analytics to understand how people find and use the site, such as which pages are visited, roughly where visitors are located, the type of device and browser, and which buttons are tapped. Google Analytics uses cookies and similar technologies to do this. We don't use it to identify you personally.

Messages you send us. If you email hello@buyunlocked.net, we keep your message and email address so we can reply.

The phone checker. The "Will it work?" checker runs in your browser. We don't store the phone or carrier you pick.

NFC charms and the GG page. Game Unlocked charms store a single web link. Tapping one opens gg.buyunlocked.net. The GG page has no sign-up and no accounts, and we don't collect personal information through it.

How we use it

We use your email address to send launch news and updates about Buy Unlocked products. We use analytics to improve the site. We use messages to answer you. We don't sell your personal information, and we don't share it for other companies' advertising.

Who else handles it

We share information only with the service providers that run the site for us: GitHub (hosting), Zoho (email list and email), and Google (analytics). Each handles data under its own privacy policy. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

Your choices

Every email we send has an unsubscribe link, and you can leave the list at any time. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or use Google's Analytics opt-out browser add-on. To ask what we hold about you, or to have it corrected or deleted, email hello@buyunlocked.net.

Children

Our email list is for people 13 and older, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you think a child under 13 has given us their information, email hello@buyunlocked.net and we will delete it.

How long we keep it

We keep your email address until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it. Analytics data is kept according to our Google Analytics retention settings.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
